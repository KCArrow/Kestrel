# Studio and commercial licensing

Kestrel is **free** for individuals, hobbyists, students, non-profits and public institutions
under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE).

Using Kestrel **at or for a company or studio** (including freelancers doing paid studio work)
needs a studio licence.

## Buy a studio licence

| Licence | Seats | Get it |
|---|---|---|
| Studio Licence | up to 25 users at a time | **[Buy on Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/s/6ee3ebf331)** |
| Studio Licence: Unlimited | any number of users, one organisation | [open an issue titled "Unlimited studio licence"](https://github.com/KCArrow/kestrel/issues/new?title=Unlimited%20studio%20licence) |

Both licences are:

- **Perpetual for Kestrel 1.x**, with every 1.x update included
- one flat price per studio: no seat counting, no licence keys, no activation, works offline
- valid for the studio's staff, and for contractors and freelancers while they work for the studio
- usable on any number of machines, at any of the studio's locations

Read the full terms before buying:
[25-user licence (PDF)](docs/licences/Kestrel-Studio-Licence-25.pdf) ·
[Unlimited licence (PDF)](docs/licences/Kestrel-Studio-Licence-Unlimited.pdf)

## After you buy

The licence PDF arrives with your Ko-fi receipt. **Keep both on file**: together they are your proof
of licence. If you bought on behalf of a studio and the receipt shows your own name, reply to the
receipt with the studio's name and you'll get written confirmation for your records.

Need an invoice for your accounts team, a purchase order, or a larger agreement? Open an issue and
we'll sort it out.

## Support and custom work

The licence covers use of the software. Support contracts, pipeline integration and custom
previewers can be arranged separately: [open an issue](https://github.com/KCArrow/kestrel/issues).
Bug reports are welcome from everyone, licensed or not: Help → Report a problem in Kestrel.
