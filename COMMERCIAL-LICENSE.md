commercial use — using Kestrel at or for a company, studio, agency or other
for-profit organization, including by freelancers doing paid work, or shipping it
as part of a product or service.

For that, a commercial licence is required. Pricing is per studio (not per seat)
and scales with team size; evaluation periods are available.

**Contact:** open an issue titled "Commercial licence" at
https://github.com/KCArrow/kestrel/issues, or reach the author through the
GitHub profile at https://github.com/KCArrow.

A commercial licence grants:

- use on any number of workstations within the licensed organization,
- internal modification and redistribution,
- priority on bug reports and feature requests,
- optional integration work (pipeline hooks, custom previewers, OpenRV/Maya/USD tooling).

The source stays public either way; the commercial licence only changes what
your organization is permitted to do with it.
